## 2025

- 2025-06-22 -- Operation Midnight Hammer. US B-2s drop 14x GBU-57A/B MOPs on Fordow, Natanz, Isfahan; sub-launched Tomahawks. Start of 12-Day War. (CRS IN12571)
- 2025-07 -- Pentagon assessment: Iran nuclear program set back ~2 years. Trump announces Iran-Israel ceasefire. (Al Jazeera 2026-02-24)

## 2026

- 2026-02-27 -- Trump authorizes Operation Epic Fury. (CNN live 2026-02-28)
- 2026-02-28 06:35 UTC -- Coordinated US-Israeli airstrikes begin (~900 strikes in 12 hrs). Israeli decapitation strike at 06:45 UTC kills Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei plus family (daughter, son-in-law, grandchild, daughter-in-law Zahra Haddad-Adel). Defense minister Aziz Nasirzadeh and ~40 senior officials killed. State media confirmed 2026-03-01. (CNN live 2026-02-28; Al Jazeera 2026-02-28)
- 2026-02-28 09:05 UTC -- Iran retaliates with ~170 ballistic missiles at Israel and Gulf states. Minab school hit (~180 civilians killed). Lamerd sports hall hit during girls' practice (>=18 killed). (CNN)
- 2026-03-01 -- Continued strikes on Tehran. Iranian missile hits Beit Shemesh synagogue (9 killed). Ali Larijani announces interim leadership council. (CNN)
- 2026-03-02 -- Hezbollah rocket barrage; IDF declares "official declaration of war." Qatar downs two Iranian Su-24s. Three US F-15s crash in Kuwait. (CNN)
- 2026-03-03 -- Mojtaba Khamenei elected Supreme Leader. US sinks IRIS Fateh (sub) and IRIS Shahid Soleimani (corvette). (CNN)
- 2026-03-04 -- F-35I downs Iranian Yak-130 -- first stealth-vs-crewed kill in history. US sub sinks frigate IRIS Dena off Sri Lanka (87 crew). (CNN)
- 2026-03-05+ -- Azadi Indoor Stadium destroyed. War expands to nine countries. (CNN)
- 2026-04-08 -- Two-week conditional ceasefire begins; deal contemplates reopening Hormuz. (House of Commons Library CBP-10636)
- 2026-04-13 -- US imposes naval blockade on Iranian ports after Islamabad Talks fail. (CNN live 2026-04-13)
- 2026-04-17 -- Iran FM Abbas Araghchi announces Strait of Hormuz open to all shipping. Trump posts on Truth Social: strait open but US blockade stays until negotiations conclude. (NPR 2026-04-19)
- 2026-04-18 -- Iran closes Hormuz again citing US "repeated breaches of trust." Guardian calls it a "dual blockade." (CNN Day 50; NPR 2026-04-18)
- 2026-04-19 -- US seizes Iranian cargo ship in Strait of Hormuz. (NPR 2026-04-19)
- 2026-04-20 -- Vessel attacks and ship seizures push ceasefire to brink. (CNBC 2026-04-20)
- 2026-04-22 -- Iran says it has seized ships in Hormuz; blockade ongoing. (NPR 2026-04-22)
- 2026-05-06 -- Both sides reportedly moving toward memo to end war; Iran reviewing US proposal. (CNN live 2026-05-06)
- 2026-05-10 -- Trump calls Iran's response "totally unacceptable." (CNN Day 72)
- 2026-05-11 -- Trump says ceasefire on "massive life support" after rejecting Tehran's counter-proposal (Hormuz sovereignty + compensation, no nuclear concessions). (CNN 2026-05-11; Al Jazeera 2026-05-11; CNBC 2026-05-11)
