## Belligerents
Coalition: United States, Israel, UAE (active); Gulf states affected by Iranian retaliation.
Iran axis: Iran, Hezbollah, Houthis, Iraqi PMF/militias.

## Casus belli (28 February 2026)
US and Israel launched coordinated surprise airstrikes during nuclear negotiations. Stated rationale: dismantle nuclear weapons program and missile capability; regime change implied but not declared. Builds on 12-Day War (June 2025) where Operation Midnight Hammer hit Fordow, Natanz, Isfahan.

## Key events (high level -- see iran-war-timeline for detail)
- 28 Feb 2026: Coalition strikes begin. Israeli decapitation strike kills Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei.
- 3 Mar 2026: Mojtaba Khamenei (his son) elected new Supreme Leader.
- 8 Apr 2026: Conditional ceasefire begins.
- 13 Apr 2026: Trump announces US naval blockade. Iran's Hormuz closure continues -> dual blockade.
- 17 Apr 2026: Iran briefly reopens Hormuz. Trump says strait open but blockade stays.
- 18 Apr 2026: Iran closes Hormuz again citing US "repeated breaches of trust."
- 11 May 2026: Trump says ceasefire on "massive life support."

## Current status (2026-05-12)
Ceasefire technically holding but fragile. Iran's counter-proposal demanded Hormuz sovereignty + compensation, did not address nuclear program. Trump called it "totally unacceptable." US demands: formal nuclear halt >=10 years, surrender of ~440 kg highly enriched uranium.

## Peace blockers
1. Hormuz: Iran refuses to reopen; US blockade remains.
2. Nuclear program: Iran has not put it on the table.
3. Failed Islamabad talks; Trump has signaled disengagement.
4. Unresolved: inspection regime, guarantees against future strikes.

## Economic impact
Largest oil supply disruption in market history (IEA: ~20M bbl/day normally transit Hormuz). Brent ~$20 above pre-war; WTI ~$10 above; US gas avg $4.52/gal (AAA). US war cost $18B (March) + $200B Pentagon request. Arab state losses $120B+. Iran damage $300B-$1T.
