When researching or updating tracked-topic memory, prefer primary news sources, scholarly literature, and official briefings over Wikipedia and Britannica.

**Why:** Davey stated this explicitly 2026-05-12. Wikipedia aggregates and can lag or carry edits of uncertain provenance.

**How to apply:**
- News: Reuters, AP, CNN, NPR, BBC, Al Jazeera, FT, WSJ, NYT, CNBC.
- Scholarly/think-tank: CFR, Brookings, RAND, Chatham House, IISS, Carnegie, ISW, CSIS, Atlantic Council; peer-reviewed journals via Google Scholar, JSTOR, arXiv.
- Official: CRS reports, House of Commons Library briefings, IAEA, UN, government white papers.
- Wikipedia/Britannica acceptable for orientation only; do not list in final Sources.
- For tracked topics, mix: news for recency, scholarly/think-tank for analysis.
